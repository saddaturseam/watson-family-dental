Watson Family Dental

Your family dentist on Watson Road

Watson Family Dental is a family and general dentistry practice at 3707 Watson Rd in St. Louis. The office grew from the 2021 merger of two practices led by friends and colleagues Dr. Stephen Christy and Dr. Jason Allen.

Care shaped by prevention

The practice emphasizes preventive care and patient education. Thorough exams look at overall tooth and gum health, include oral cancer exams, and use x-rays when needed. Treatment planning considers restorative and cosmetic options for your dental health.

Care when concerns arise

If a dental emergency occurs, the practice makes every effort to see and care for you as soon as possible. Call the office to discuss your situation.
